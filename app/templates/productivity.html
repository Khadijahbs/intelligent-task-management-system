What Your Metrics Mean

Completion rate shows how much of your recorded workload has been completed. On-time completion indicates how consistently completed tasks meet their deadlines, while pending workload shows the amount of unfinished work.

💡

Productivity Insight

Maintaining a high completion rate while completing tasks on time can contribute to stronger productivity performance. The system uses these behavioural indicators together with historical task data to support productivity classification.

How Productivity Classification Works

The system analyses historical task activity to support productivity classification. The machine-learning model uses task completion rate, on-time completion rate, pending workload, and total task information. The machine-learning component classifies productivity into three levels: High, Moderate, or Low.

Important: The productivity classification becomes available after sufficient completed task history has been recorded. This helps prevent the system from making a classification from too little user-specific data.
